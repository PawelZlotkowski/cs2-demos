How a review is made

Eight stages, the same ones you watch on the processing screen. The four on the right run on our own hardware; no hosted AI service sees your match.
1UploadA .dem from CS Demo Manager or a .dem.zst from FACEIT.
2Parsedemoparser2 reads every tick: positions, shots, damage, grenades and the bomb.
3Choose a playerYou pick one player, a focus and a language. Everything after is about that player.
4DetectTen detectors turn events into findings with IDs such as F07. Plain code, no model.
ON OUR RTX 5080 · QWEN3-14B · NO HOSTED AI
5Select momentsThe coach agent picks five or six moments from findings and round stats. A code ranker takes over if it fails.
6ExplainThe model reads the match only through MCP tools and writes each explanation in English or Polish.
7VerifyEvery number and ID is checked against the findings. A claim that does not match is repaired or dropped.
8RecordCS Demo Manager records each moment from your view. The radar is ready before the clips.

Why you can trust what it says

Numbers come from code The model never invents a number. Speeds, times and counts come from the parser and the detectors, and each claim cites the finding it came from.
The evidence is one click away Click F07 or any time in an answer and the replay jumps to 3 s before it and pauses, so you can judge it yourself.
Honest when a part is offline If the model or the recorder is off, the review still opens with the radar and template explanations, and says so on the page.
