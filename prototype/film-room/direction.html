ROUND REVIEWER
Visual direction · Film Room · 15 November 2026 · for the 25 November showcase

The replay is lit. Everything else steps back.

A film room for one player: one stage, five or six chosen moments, and a coach whose every claim points at the second of the match that proves it. Premium comes from restraint and proof, not decoration.

What a teacher should notice in the first minute

It is about CS2 at a glance. The scorebug, radar and round timeline carry the identity, not a logo.

The AI is grounded. Each claim cites a finding and shows that the verifier checked it.

It is honest. Real pipeline stages and counts, designed offline and error states.

It feels made. Chrome without hue, two typefaces, one motion language.

Principles

Stage first
Radar or clip takes the leftover width and the brightest pixels. Side columns stay narrow and can hide. No dashboard modules.
Evidence on everything
Claims carry IDs such as F07. One gesture everywhere: click any evidence and the replay seeks to 3 s before it and pauses.
Colour means something
The chrome has no hue. Orange triangle for a mistake, blue dot for a good play, game colours only on the stage. When colour appears, the coach found something.
Two typefaces, strict roles
Archivo condensed for numbers, clocks and codes. Hanken Grotesk for every word, including the coach.
Honest states
Real stage names, real counts and timings, no fake percentages. Empty, offline and failed states are designed as carefully as success.
