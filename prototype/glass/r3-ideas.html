Round 3 research, 15 November

Glass over the map. Soft, precise, and only colourful where it means something.

You asked for frosted glass, round edges, popping colour and a look that stands apart from everything earlier. These boards answer with one idea in three colour worlds: the radar becomes a physical model that fills the screen, and all controls float above it as glass.

What you have already told me you like

Your earlier app briefPrecise, technical, premium, priced like WHOOP. Soft corners, smooth springs, summary first and detail on tap. Cheap tells you listed: purple gradients, emoji icons, rings everywhere, every section in a rounded card, fake-precise percentages.
Your earlier design rulesGlass belongs to floating bars, never to reading content. Continuous corners, closer to Apple's, with inner shapes concentric to outer ones. Controls are capsules. Springs bounce once at most.
Your island feedbackTwo scores should stand apart by their own tinted tile, not by a thin divider. Everything should feel like it belongs together.
This requestFrosted glass and round edges like the @uiuxjosh posts, bone with pink or light and dark blue with yellow, no squares, nothing that looks like a 70s calendar.

Three colour worlds

Porcelain
13–7 Bone porcelain chrome around a dark game-screen radar in Counter-Strike colours, riso pink for you. Mona Sans in one family: expanded for numbers, normal for words. Chosen on 15 November.
Daylight
13:7 Ice blue with a cobalt pool and a yellow you. Geologica for words, Doto for the LED clock. The most striking on a projector.
Night match
13/7 Navy, not black, with butter yellow. Science Gothic for numbers, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading. Best as the dark mode of Daylight.

Rules every board follows

Glass only on what floats: nav, dock, island, lens. The coach reads on a near-solid sheet. Outer corners 28 px, inner 18 px, controls as capsules. No hairline dividers: spacing and tinted tiles instead. Colour means something: the accent is you, red or orange is a mistake, green is a good play. No all-caps labels, no dot-separated meta strings, no glow, no rings except a real progress state. Springs with no bounce for UI, one small bounce only after a flick. Keyboard and reduced motion jump instantly.
