    How impact works

    Impact adds up what a player did in a round using facts from the demo. It is a simple, readable estimate, not a win-probability model, so use it to find rounds worth reviewing rather than to rank players.

    The round's lobby average is then subtracted, so 0 is an average round in this match and the ten players' scores add up to 0. A trade is a kill within 5 s of the victim killing your teammate. Damage counts enemy health only, at most 100 per enemy per round.
