# Anubis utility

Purpose and timing of the common grenades. Lineups are left out on purpose; learn them in-game.

## Mid smokes

Zones: Mid, Canal, Bridge, Top mid.
A T smoke on the CT view of mid lets Ts take the canal and the bridge without facing an AWP. CTs use a molotov or a smoke to stop a fast mid take. Mid is where most early utility is spent on Anubis.

## A site smokes

Zones: CT to A, A connector, A site.
Smokes on CT to A and A connector isolate A site during an execute. They stop rotations and cut the two angles a retake would use.

## A main flashes and molotovs

Zones: A main, A site.
Ts flash over the end of A main just before the entry peeks. CTs molotov A main to delay a push. A molotov that forces the anchor to move is usually worth more than a flash thrown too early.

## B site smokes

Zones: B CT, B connector, B site.
Smokes on B CT and B connector block CT rotations and the retake path during a B execute. With both smokes down, Ts only have to clear the close corners of the site.

## B main molotov

Zones: B main, B long.
CTs throw a molotov into B main to delay a push. Ts waiting in B main should back off instead of burning. After it burns out, the push should come with flashes.

## Canal and water utility

Zones: Canal, A water.
Movement in the water is slow and loud, which makes a flash or molotov on a player crossing it very strong. Ts crossing the canal or A water should do so behind a smoke.

## Keep utility for the post-plant

Zones: A site, B site.
A molotov and a smoke kept for after the plant stop a defuse or delay a retake. Dying with grenades in hand means the team played the round with fewer tools than it had.
