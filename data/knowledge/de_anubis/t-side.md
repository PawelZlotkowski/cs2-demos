# Anubis T side

## Default: mid first

Zones: Mid, Canal, Bridge, Top mid.
Anubis defaults usually start with mid. Ts take Mid and the canal with a smoke on the CT view, then decide whether to push Top mid or split. Mid control gives access to both connectors, so it is the key to Anubis T sides.

## Default: site presence

Zones: B long, B main, A main, A water.
While mid is being taken, one player shows on B main and one on A main or A water to learn how many CTs play each site. These players should stay out of CT molotovs and avoid isolated duels.

## A execute

Zones: A main, A water, A site, CT to A, A connector.
The standard A execute smokes CT to A and A connector, flashes out of A main, and brings a second group from A water. Entering from two sides splits the CT's attention. The plant should be behind cover that hides it from CT to A.

## A split through A connector

Zones: Top mid, A connector, A site, A main.
After taking Top mid, two Ts go through A connector while the rest come from A main. The anchor is caught between them. The mid group needs a smoke on the CT spawn side so it is not shot from behind.

## B execute

Zones: B long, B main, B site, B CT, B connector.
The standard B execute smokes B CT and B connector, uses a molotov on the close corner of the site and flashes over the B main exit. Ts enter together; a B execute that comes out one player at a time loses the first duel and stalls.

## B split through B connector

Zones: Top mid, B connector, B site, B main.
With mid under control, two Ts come through B connector while the B main group enters. The B anchor then faces two entrances. Timing matters: both groups should reach the site within a few seconds of each other.

## Fast B rush

Zones: B long, B main, B site.
A fast B rush can beat a CT side that plays only one player on B. It works on ecos and after CT rotations have been slow. The first player needs a flash; a CT molotov in B main stops the rush if thrown in time.

## Lurking

Zones: Canal, Mid, A water, B long.
A lurker stays on the other side of the execute to catch rotators or to call when CTs leave. The canal is a common lurk route but loud. A lurker's value comes from timing and information, not from early duels.

## Eco and force rounds

Zones: B main, A main.
On an eco, stacking one entrance and fighting at close range gives pistols and SMGs a chance. Spreading across the map on an eco gives CTs easy one-on-one fights with better weapons.

## Post-plant on A

Zones: A site, A main, A water, A connector.
After an A plant, Ts hold from A main, A water and A connector so the retake has to cross several lines. A molotov saved for the defuse is the strongest tool.

## Post-plant on B

Zones: B site, B main, B connector.
After a B plant, Ts play from B main and B connector and keep one player watching B CT. Spreading out too far means players die one at a time to a grouped retake.
