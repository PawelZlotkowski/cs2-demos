# Anubis callouts

One section per zone in `apps/api/app/maps/zones/de_anubis.json`. Directions follow the radar: CT spawn at the top, T spawn at the bottom, A on the right, B on the left. Sub-callouts differ between teams; the zone names are what findings use.

## T spawn

Zones: T spawn.
The Terrorist spawn at the south of the map. Routes lead left to B long and B main, up the middle to Mid and the canals, and right towards A water and A main. Deaths here are rare and usually mean a CT push or a lurker being caught late.

## B long

Zones: B long.
The first stretch of the western route out of T spawn, leading to B main. Ts gather here before a B hit. A CT molotov or an early push through B main can catch a group standing still.

## B main

Zones: B main.
The main T entrance to B site. Ts leaving B main face CTs on the site and from B CT, so a B entry needs smokes on the CT side and flashes over the entrance. It is the most common place for B executes to stall.

## B site

Zones: B site.
The B bombsite, with a pillar and several boxes for cover. CTs hold it from the back of the site and from B CT; Ts plant behind cover so the bomb cannot be seen from the retake routes. The site is open to B main, B connector and B CT at the same time.

## B CT

Zones: B CT.
The CT side of B site, between the site and CT spawn. CT rotations and retakes on B come through here. A smoke here during a B execute cuts rotations and one of the main holding angles.

## B connector

Zones: B connector.
The passage linking Top mid with B site. CTs use it to rotate between Mid and B; Ts who win Mid use it to split into B from behind the anchor. A player dying here is often caught between two sides.

## Mid

Zones: Mid.
The T-side end of mid, just out of T spawn. Ts gather here to go through the canals or to cross towards A water. Early mid control decides whether Ts can split later.

## Canal

Zones: Canal.
The water channel in the middle of the map. Players in the water are slower and easy to hear, so crossing it without a smoke or a flash is risky. Ts use it to reach the bridge and Top mid, and to lurk.

## Bridge

Zones: Bridge.
The bridge over the canal between Mid and Top mid. It is a crossing point both teams watch. Standing on the bridge exposes a player to Top mid and the canal at once.

## Top mid

Zones: Top mid.
The CT side of mid, near CT spawn, joining B connector on one side and A connector on the other. A CT here watches the whole mid lane. Ts who take Top mid can split to either site, so CTs contest it with utility and AWPs.

## A water

Zones: A water.
The watery area between Mid and A main on the right side of the map. Ts use it to reach A without taking A main, and to join an A execute from the side. As in the canal, movement in the water is slow and loud.

## A main

Zones: A main.
The main T entrance to A site on the right. It ends in the open towards the site, where CTs can hold from several spots. A entries from A main should come with smokes on the CT side and flashes over the corner.

## A connector

Zones: A connector.
The route between Top mid and A site. CTs rotate through it from mid to A; Ts use it to split onto A from behind. A smoke here cuts mid rotations during an A execute.

## A site

Zones: A site.
The A bombsite in the north-east. It is open to A main, A connector and CT to A, so a lone CT can be caught from several angles. Ts need to clear the close corners and smoke the CT approach before planting.

## CT to A

Zones: CT to A.
The path from CT spawn to A site. Retakes on A come through here. A T smoke on this path is the standard way to stop rotations and to plant safely.

## CT spawn

Zones: CT spawn.
The Counter-Terrorist spawn at the top of the map, between both sites. From here CTs reach B through B CT, A through CT to A, and mid through Top mid. Retakes usually group near spawn before splitting into the site.
