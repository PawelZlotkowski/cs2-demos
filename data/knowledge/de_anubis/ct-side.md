# Anubis CT side

## Standard setup

Zones: A site, A connector, Top mid, B connector, B site.
A common setup is two on A, one or two in mid (Top mid or a connector), and one or two on B. The mid players rotate to whichever site is hit. Changing the setup between rounds keeps Ts from reading it.

## A anchor

Zones: A site, A main, CT to A.
The A anchor delays A main with utility and falls back towards CT to A if the execute is full. Dying first without using utility gives Ts the site for free. Staying alive for the retake is often worth more than one early kill.

## B anchor

Zones: B site, B main, B CT.
The B anchor holds B main from the site and throws a molotov when Ts push. Against a full execute, the anchor should call, delay and fall back to B CT, not trade one for one on the site.

## Mid player

Zones: Top mid, Bridge, Canal, A connector, B connector.
The mid CT watches the canal and the bridge and calls when Ts take mid. They must not be caught alone: if Ts kill the mid player during their mid take, that opens both connectors. An AWP in mid is common but needs a partner to trade.

## Early aggression

Zones: Bridge, Canal, B main, A main.
CTs can push the bridge or a main early to gain information and punish slow defaults. Such plays need a teammate ready to trade. An early push that dies untraded gives Ts a five against four for the rest of the round.

## Stacking

Zones: A site, B site.
When Ts repeat the same site, CTs can stack it. The player left alone on the other site must play safe and call, not fight.

## Retake A

Zones: CT to A, A connector, A site.
CTs group at CT to A and A connector, smoke off A main and flash in together. Coming in one by one from different directions loses retakes.

## Retake B

Zones: B CT, B connector, B site.
Retakes on B come from B CT and B connector. Smoke B main to cut post-plant players and flash over the site before entering in pairs.

## Saving

Zones: CT spawn.
When the retake numbers are hopeless, saving a rifle for the next round is correct. A save is not a mistake when the odds are against the CTs.
