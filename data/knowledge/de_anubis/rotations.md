# Anubis rotations

## CT rotation between sites

Zones: CT spawn, CT to A, B CT.
CT spawn sits between the sites, so rotations through spawn are shorter than on many maps. CTs should still rotate on the first clear commitment, not after the plant, or they arrive one at a time into crossfires.

## Rotating through the connectors

Zones: Top mid, A connector, B connector.
The connectors are the fastest path between mid and each site. Rotating through them is risky if Ts hold Top mid, because the rotator can be shot in the side.

## T rotation through the canal

Zones: Mid, Canal, A water, B long.
Ts switch sites through Mid and the canal or back through T spawn. The canal is quicker but loud. A late T rotation gives CTs time to rotate as well.

## Reading rotations

Zones: Bridge, Top mid, CT spawn.
A T lurker on the bridge or in the canal can see or hear CTs moving through Top mid. Calling the rotation lets the team hit the site that was left weak.

## Mid player timing

Zones: Top mid, A connector, B connector.
The mid CT is the first rotator. Rotating on a fake leaves the mid open and gives Ts a free split, so the mid player should wait for a real commitment (several Ts or utility on one site).
