## A site

Terrorists reach A through A main and A water, and from mid through A connector. The site is open, so the attackers need smokes on the defender positions towards CT before crossing. Defenders often hold A with a crossfire from the site and a player further back in CT to A.

## B site

Terrorists reach B through B main and B long, and from mid through B connector. B is a common first target because B main opens straight onto the site. Defenders hold from the site and B CT, and molotovs in B main delay an early push.

## Mid, Canal and Bridge

Mid, Canal and Bridge connect both sides of the map and are where many early fights happen. Control of mid lets attackers rotate between sites quickly or split. A defender holding mid alone should expect to be pushed with utility and fall back before being traded out.

## CT rotations on Anubis

Rotations between A and B are shorter on the CT side than for the attackers who have to cross mid. Defenders can fall back and retake instead of holding alone. After a plant, grouping up before the retake matters more than arriving first.
