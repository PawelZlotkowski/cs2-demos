## Trading a teammate

A trade is killing the enemy who just killed your teammate before that enemy can reposition. It only works if the second player is close enough to see the same angle within a second or two. When you take a fight, a teammate should be right behind you or holding the angle your killer will be on. Dying where nobody can trade you gives the enemy a free kill and the man advantage.

## Isolated deaths

Dying far from your team usually means you took a fight nobody could follow up. Lurking and holding a site alone are valid roles, but they need information and an escape route. If you die alone in the same area again and again, ask whether you should play closer to a teammate, fall back after first contact, or wait for utility.

## Crossfires

A crossfire is two players watching the same area from different angles, so an enemy who peeks one of them is exposed to the other. On defence it is the main way to win fights you would lose alone. On attack, clear one angle at a time or blind one of the two players with a flash before you go in.

## Moving while shooting

Rifles, snipers and pistols are only accurate when you are standing still or moving very slowly. Shots fired while running spread widely, so stop before you shoot. Counter-strafing means tapping the opposite movement key to stop at once. Crouching or walking does not fix running inaccuracy on its own.

## Dry peeks

A dry peek is walking into a fight without a flash, a smoke or a teammate's help. The defender who is already aiming at the angle wins most of these fights. Before peeking a held angle, get a flash from yourself or a teammate, take a wider or unexpected angle, or wait for the enemy to reveal their position.

## Dying with utility

Grenades you die with are money spent for nothing. If you are about to take a fight, throw your flash or molotov first. If you are holding a site, use the molotov to delay a push before you are overrun. Dying with a full set of grenades often means you peeked too early or held on to utility "for later".

## Flashing teammates

A flash that blinds your own team gives the enemy a free fight. Call your flashes, throw them so they pop behind or above your teammates, and do not throw one into a space a teammate is about to run through. Pop flashes that burst just as they clear a wall are harder to turn away from for enemies and easier to plan around for your team.

## Buying with the team

Economy works as a team: if one player buys rifles while the rest save, that player usually loses the round alone and the team is poorer next round. Match your team's call. Save together when money is low so everyone can buy together next round, and do not force-buy alone unless the team agrees.

## Rotating after a plant

On the defending side, once the bomb is planted the round is about getting to the site together before the timer runs out. Rotating late, or one by one, lets the attackers take each defender in turn. Group up with teammates near the site, use utility to clear angles and retake together.

## Opening duels

The first kill of the round decides a large share of rounds, because the team with five players can trade and take space. Taking an opening duel is fine when it is your role and you have support. Losing it without a trade or information puts the team four versus five.
