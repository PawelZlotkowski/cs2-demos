## A site from the T side

Terrorists reach A through A ramp, Palace, and from mid through Connector or Jungle. A split usually sends players from ramp and Palace at the same time so that defenders cannot watch both. Defenders commonly hold from Jungle, A CT, Stairs and Tetris, so a take needs smokes on Jungle and CT and flashes over the site.

## Palace

Palace is a narrow route from the T side onto A site. A player leaving Palace is seen from several defender angles at once, so leaving alone without a flash usually loses the fight. Palace is also a common lurk spot late in rounds.

## Mid control

Mid connects both sites. Terrorists take mid through Top mid to reach Connector and Short. Defenders watch from Window and Connector. A smoke on Window lets attackers cross mid safely; winning mid lets the T side split onto A through Connector or onto B through Short.

## B site and B apartments

Terrorists reach B through T apartments and B apartments or from mid through Short. The exit from B apartments into the site is narrow and easy to hold with a molotov and a crossfire from the site and Market. A B take usually needs a molotov on the default positions and flashes over the B apartments exit.

## CT rotations on Mirage

Defenders rotate between A and B through CT spawn, Market, Jungle and Connector. A defender rotating through Connector can be caught by attackers who already hold mid, so the safer route depends on who controls mid.
