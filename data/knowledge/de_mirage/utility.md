# Mirage utility

Purpose and timing of the common grenades. Exact lineups are left out on purpose: they change with patches and are best learned in-game.

## Window smoke

Zones: Window, Top mid, Mid.
Thrown from T spawn or Top mid to block the Window player's view down Mid. It is the first grenade in most T rounds because it lets Ts take Top mid and Underpass safely. Without it, crossing Mid against an AWP is a coin flip.

## Connector and Jungle smokes

Zones: Connector, Jungle.
A Connector smoke from Mid cuts CT rotations to A. A Jungle smoke from T side blocks the side angle on A ramp and Palace during an A execute. Together with the CT smoke they isolate A site.

## CT smoke

Zones: A CT, A site.
Blocks the approach from CT spawn onto A site. It stops rotators and makes the plant safer. It is part of every standard A execute.

## Stairs smoke

Zones: Stairs, A site.
Blocks a CT holding A ramp and Palace from Stairs. Used with the Jungle and CT smokes in the A execute.

## Firebox and Tetris molotovs

Zones: A site, Tetris, Palace.
Ts use molotovs to clear CTs from Firebox, Tetris or Ninja before entering A. CTs use a molotov on A ramp or Palace to delay an execute. A molotov that forces a CT to move is often worth more than a flash.

## A ramp and Palace flashes

Zones: A ramp, Palace, A site.
Pop flashes thrown over the Palace exit or out of A ramp blind CTs on the site just as the entry player peeks. A flash must be thrown so it blinds the enemy, not the entry player; a flash thrown straight ahead into the site often hits teammates.

## B site smokes

Zones: Market, Short, B site.
Smokes on Market window, Market door and Short cut CT vision and rotations during a B execute. Ts can plant behind Pillars once the three smokes land.

## B apartments molotov

Zones: B apartments, B site.
CTs throw a molotov into the apartments exit to delay a push through the balcony. Ts waiting in apartments should back off and not burn in it. After it burns out, the push can come with flashes.

## Short smoke and flash

Zones: Short, Mid.
A smoke on Short protects Ts crossing Mid and blocks a CT holding from Short. CTs flash Short to hold it aggressively. Short fights are close, so a well-timed flash usually decides them.

## Save utility for after the plant

Zones: A site, B site.
Keeping one molotov and one smoke for after the plant stops a defuse or delays a retake. Many rounds are lost with Ts holding grenades they never used. Dying with unused utility means the team fought the round with fewer tools than it had.
