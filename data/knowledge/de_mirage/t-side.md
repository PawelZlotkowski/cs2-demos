# Mirage T side

## Default: map control first

Zones: Top mid, A ramp, B apartments, Mid.
A standard Mirage T default takes Top mid early (after a Window smoke), puts one player in A ramp or Palace for pressure, and one in B apartments for information. The goal is to learn where CTs are before committing. Ts who die alone early in the default give CTs a free man advantage, so each position should be tradeable or safe to leave.

## Default: what each position learns

Zones: Palace, A ramp, B apartments, Top mid.
Palace and A ramp tell the team how many CTs are on A and whether they use utility early. B apartments shows whether CTs push or play passive on B. Top mid and Underpass reveal the Window and Short players. Calling this information early lets the in-game leader pick a site with 40 to 50 seconds left.

## A execute

Zones: A ramp, Palace, A site, Jungle, Stairs, A CT.
The standard A execute smokes CT, Jungle and Stairs, mollies Firebox or Tetris, and flashes over from A ramp and Palace. Ts then enter from ramp and Palace at the same time so a CT cannot hold both. Entry players should go as soon as the smokes bloom; waiting lets CTs play around them.

## A split through Connector

Zones: Connector, Jungle, A site, Mid.
After taking Mid, two or three Ts go through Connector into Jungle while the rest come from ramp and Palace. The split puts a CT on A in a crossfire from behind. It needs a smoke on Window or Short so the Connector group is not shot in the back.

## B execute through apartments

Zones: B apartments, B site, Market, Short.
The standard B execute smokes Market window, Market door and Short, mollies Bench or Van, and flashes over the apartments balcony. Ts leave apartments together and plant behind Pillars or in the open once Short and Market are blocked.

## B split through Short

Zones: Short, Mid, B apartments, B site.
Ts who control Mid can send two players up Short while the apartments group enters together with them. The CT on B then has to watch two entrances. A Window smoke keeps the Short group safe from a CT AWP in Window.

## Fast B apartments rush

Zones: T apartments, B apartments, B site.
A fast rush of four or five through apartments can beat a CT side that plays two or three on A and Mid. It works best after a loss that reset CT habits or on an eco. The risk is a CT molotov in apartments; the first player should carry a flash to clear the balcony.

## Mid control for a late split

Zones: Top mid, Mid, Connector, Short.
Holding Mid until the CT rotator has committed opens a late split into either site. Ts need a smoke for Window and one spare for Connector or Short. Late splits fail when players arrive one by one, so the group should agree on a time to go.

## Lurking

Zones: Mid loop, B apartments, Palace, Top mid.
A lurker stays on the opposite side of the team's execute to catch rotating CTs or to cut off a retake. Palace and B apartments are common lurk spots. A lurker who dies alone early without information is a lost player; the lurker's job is timing and survival, not early kills.

## Eco and force rounds

Zones: T apartments, Palace, B apartments.
On an eco, Ts often stack one site to get close-range fights where pistols and SMGs work, and to pick up rifles. Spreading out on an eco turns every fight into a one against one with worse weapons. A good eco goal is to take one or two kills and damage the CT economy.

## Post-plant on A

Zones: A site, Palace, A ramp, Stairs, Jungle.
After a Default plant, Ts hold from Palace, A ramp and Stairs so the defuser has to cross several lines. A player in Jungle or on Connector can cut rotations. Save a molotov or a smoke for the defuse; throwing all utility before the plant leaves nothing to stop a retake.

## Post-plant on B

Zones: B site, B apartments, Short, Market.
After a B plant, Ts often play from B apartments, Short and Underpass so the retake has to come through Market and Arches into crossfires. A molotov on the bomb during the defuse is the strongest post-plant tool on B.
