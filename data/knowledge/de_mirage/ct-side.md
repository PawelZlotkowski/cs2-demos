# Mirage CT side

## Standard setup

Zones: A site, Connector, Jungle, Window, Short, B site.
A common CT setup is two on A (one site anchor, one in Jungle or Connector), one mid player in Window or Connector, and two on B (one site anchor, one on Short or Market). The mid player is the rotator who can help either site. Setups change round to round so Ts cannot read them.

## A anchor

Zones: A site, Stairs, Firebox, A CT.
The A anchor delays the execute, uses utility on A ramp and Palace, and falls back to CT or Jungle to wait for help instead of dying on the site. An anchor who dies in the first contact without utility leaves A open. Surviving to retake is often worth more than one early kill.

## Jungle and Connector player

Zones: Jungle, Connector, Mid.
This player watches A from the side and Mid through Connector. They should call when Ts cross Mid, because a Connector split behind the A anchor is the main danger. Playing too far out in Mid without a teammate to trade is a common way to die for nothing.

## Window and Short player

Zones: Window, Short, Mid.
The mid CT, often with an AWP, holds Mid from Window or Short. Once a Window smoke lands, they should reposition rather than wait for Ts to walk through it. Moving between Window, Short and Connector keeps Ts guessing.

## B anchor

Zones: B site, Market, B apartments.
The B anchor holds the apartments exit from Van, Bench or Market and uses a molotov on the balcony when Ts push. B is small and hard to hold alone against five, so the anchor should delay, call, and fall back to Market or Arches for the retake.

## Early aggression

Zones: Short, Palace, B apartments, Mid.
CTs can take early fights by peeking Top mid, pushing Palace or pushing apartments. These plays gain information and punish slow T defaults, but they must be timed and supported. An early push that dies untraded gives Ts a five against four.

## Stacking a site

Zones: A site, B site.
When Ts have hit the same site several rounds in a row, CTs can stack it with three or four players. Stacks are strong but risky, so the lone player on the other site should stay alive and give information rather than fight.

## Retake A

Zones: A CT, Jungle, Connector, A site.
CTs group at CT and Connector, use smokes to cut Palace or ramp, and flash in together. Coming into A one by one from different sides is the most common failed retake. With the bomb planted on Default, clear Stairs and Firebox before the defuse.

## Retake B

Zones: Market, Arches, Short, B site.
Retakes on B come from Market, Arches and Short. Smoke the apartments balcony and flash over Market before entering. Trade in pairs and make sure someone checks Short and Van before tapping the defuse.

## Saving

Zones: CT spawn, Arches, Market.
If a retake has no realistic chance (for example two against four with little time), saving a rifle protects the next round's buy. A saved weapon is worth more than a lost gunfight, and a save is not a mistake when the numbers are against the CTs.
