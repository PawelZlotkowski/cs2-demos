# Mirage callouts

One section per zone in `apps/api/app/maps/zones/de_mirage.json`. The zone name is the section title, so findings that say "died in Palace" retrieve the matching section.

## T spawn

Zones: T spawn.
The Terrorist spawn on the east side of the map. Three routes leave it: up T ramp towards A and Palace, through the mid doors to Top mid, and along the north corridor to T apartments for B. Players rarely fight here. Deaths in T spawn usually mean an aggressive CT push or a late lurker being caught.

## T ramp

Zones: T ramp.
The slope from T spawn down towards the A ramp and the Palace entrance. It is the staging area for A executes. CTs can throw molotovs and grenades at the bottom of the ramp from A site, so Ts should not stand still here while waiting for a call.

## Palace

Zones: Palace.
The upstairs corridor from T side into the east edge of A site, ending on a balcony above A ramp. From the Palace exit a T sees A site, Tetris and the stairs. CTs often hold it from Firebox, Tetris or from inside with a shotgun. A molotov or a well-timed flash clears the exit. Palace gives an elevated second angle for A executes together with A ramp.

## A ramp

Zones: A ramp.
The main T entrance to A site, running west from T ramp. Important spots along it: Tetris on the right, Palace above on the left, Sandwich and Stairs further in, and the A site boxes (Triple, Firebox, Default). Ts coming out of A ramp face several CT angles at once, so executes smoke CT and Jungle and flash over before anyone peeks.

## Stairs

Zones: Stairs.
The stairs on the west side of A site that lead up to Jungle and Connector. A CT on Stairs holds A ramp and Palace and can drop back up to Jungle. Ts smoke Stairs together with CT and Jungle in the standard A execute. Note for reviewers: the draft zone polygon places Stairs near Palace; check it against the overlay in `docs/coach/zones/`.

## A site

Zones: A site.
The bombsite. Common boxes: Default (the usual plant spot, in the middle of the site), Triple (stacked boxes near Palace), Firebox (the corner near Palace and Stairs), Ninja (a hidden corner below Palace), Sandwich (between Stairs and the site), and the Ticket booth near CT. Default plants are safest when CT, Jungle and Stairs are smoked or cleared.

## Tetris

Zones: Tetris.
Stacked boxes on the inside of A ramp, where ramp opens to the site. A CT can play Tetris to catch Ts as they walk out of ramp, but it is easy to trade from Palace and ramp. Ts clear it with a molotov or by jumping onto it early in an execute.

## A CT

Zones: A CT.
The CT side of A site, between the site and CT spawn, including the Ticket booth. CTs rotate through here and retakes from CT spawn start here. For Ts, a smoke on CT (the "CT smoke") cuts this whole approach during an A execute.

## Jungle

Zones: Jungle.
The area at the top of Stairs between Connector and A site. A CT in Jungle covers A ramp and Palace from the side and can fall back to Connector or CT. Ts smoke Jungle together with CT in a standard A execute. Stairs and Jungle together form a crossfire on A ramp.

## Connector

Zones: Connector.
The corridor linking Jungle and A site with Mid and Window. It lets CTs move between A and Mid quickly, and lets Ts split into A from Mid. A CT in Connector watches Mid and Short. A Connector smoke from T side stops CT rotations from Mid to A.

## CT spawn

Zones: CT spawn.
The Counter-Terrorist spawn west of A site. From here CTs reach A through A CT and B through Market and Arches. Retakes are usually grouped here or in Market before going in together.

## Arches

Zones: Arches.
The passage from CT spawn north towards Market and B site, with a window onto the mid area. CTs rotating from A to B go through Arches. It is also where the CT B rotator waits to help Market or Short.

## Top mid

Zones: Top mid.
The T side end of Mid, just out of T spawn, with boxes near the entrance. Ts take Top mid control early to open Mid, Connector, Short and Window. CTs with an AWP in Window can see Top mid, which is why Ts smoke Window early.

## Mid

Zones: Mid.
The open central area between Top mid and Short and Connector, including Underpass (the tunnel below Mid that leads to B apartments) and Chair (a spot on the CT side). Controlling Mid lets Ts split to A via Connector or to B via Short. Players crossing Mid are visible from Window, Short and Connector at once.

## Window

Zones: Window.
The raised room on the CT side of Mid with a view down the whole of Mid. A CT AWP often plays Window. Ts smoke it early (the "Window smoke") to cross Mid safely. Window also connects to Short and to Market for fast rotations.

## Short

Zones: Short.
Also called Catwalk. The raised walkway from Mid to B site. Ts use it to split into B with the B apartments group; CTs hold it from B site, Market window or Window. Short fights happen at close range, so utility and trading matter.

## T apartments

Zones: T apartments.
The first rooms after T spawn on the north route to B. Ts gather here before an apartments push. Deaths here usually mean a CT pushed through B apartments early or a molotov caught a group standing still.

## Mid loop

Zones: Mid loop.
The small loop between T spawn, T apartments and Top mid. It lets Ts move between the Mid and B apartments routes without being seen. Lurkers use it to fake one side and arrive at the other.

## B apartments

Zones: B apartments.
The upstairs building that ends on a balcony overlooking B site. This is the main T entrance to B. CTs molotov the exit and hold it from Van, Bench or Short. Ts leaving apartments should flash over the balcony and have a second player ready to trade.

## B site

Zones: B site.
The B bombsite. Spots: Van (the vehicle at the back), Bench (the corner near Market), Pillars, Short (the walkway coming in from Mid), Market door and Market window on the CT side. Planting behind the pillars hides the bomb from Market. B is small, so one grenade can clear a spot.

## Market

Zones: Market.
The shop building between B site and CT, with a door and a window onto B. CTs use it to rotate and to hold B from cover. For retakes, CTs group in Market or Arches before entering B together. A smoke on Market window and door blocks CT vision during a B execute.
