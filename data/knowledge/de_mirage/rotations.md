# Mirage rotations

Times are rough and depend on weapon and route. They are for comparing, not quoting.

## CT rotation from A to B

Zones: A CT, CT spawn, Arches, Market, B site.
CTs on A rotate to B through CT spawn, Arches and Market. The route is long, so the A players should rotate as soon as B calls a commitment, not after the plant. A late rotation arrives one player at a time into T crossfires.

## CT rotation from B to A

Zones: Market, Arches, CT spawn, A CT, Jungle.
B players rotate to A through Market, Arches and CT, or through Short and Connector if Mid is safe. Rotating through Mid is faster but risky if Ts hold it.

## Mid player rotation

Zones: Window, Short, Connector, Jungle, Market.
The mid player is closest to both sites and usually rotates first. From Window they reach B through Short or Market, and A through Connector and Jungle. If they rotate too early on a fake, the other site loses its help.

## T rotation through Mid and Underpass

Zones: Top mid, Mid, Mid loop, T apartments.
Ts switch sites through Mid loop and Top mid or through Underpass to B apartments. A T rotation after contact gives CTs time to rotate as well, so it works best when the first site has shown CT utility and players were not lost.

## Reading a rotation

Zones: Connector, Market, Arches.
Ts can hear or see rotations through Connector and Market. A lurker in Palace or B apartments who calls the rotation lets the team execute the weaker site. CTs who rotate silently and early take away that information.
