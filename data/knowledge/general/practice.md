# Practice drills

One drill per mistake detector, for the end-of-review wrap-up (design plan item 3). The first line names the detector, like the fundamentals, so the wrap-up can find the drill for each mistake type.

## Practise counter-strafing

Detectors: shot_while_moving.
On an aim map with bots, strafe left and right and only click after tapping the opposite key to stop. Start slowly and say "stop, shoot" out loud until the stop is automatic. Then add a rule: any shot fired while the movement keys are held counts as a miss. Ten minutes before each session is enough.

## Practise peeking with help

Detectors: dry_peek.
In a private server, learn one pop flash for each angle you usually take first on your side of the map. Before every contested peek in your next matches, ask yourself whether a flash, a smoke or a teammate is ready; if none is, jiggle peek for information instead of fighting.

## Practise staying tradeable

Detectors: untraded_death.
In your next matches, before each fight, check where your nearest teammate is on the radar. If they could not swing on your killer within a second or two, fall back or wait for them. Review two deaths per match afterwards and ask who could have traded each one.

## Practise using utility before fights

Detectors: unused_utility.
Pick two grenades per side of the map that you will always throw before your first fight, and learn them in a private server. In matches, set yourself a rule: if you die holding a grenade you bought, that round counts as a mistake in your notes.

## Practise safer flashes

Detectors: team_flash.
In a private server with a teammate or a bot, practise pop flashes that burst behind the corner and out of your team's view. Say the flash before you throw it, and learn which of your usual flashes blind the player next to you.

## Practise opening duels

Detectors: opening_duel.
Play prefire practice maps for your map until you clear the common angles with your crosshair already at head height. In matches, take the first duel only with help or from an angle you have practised; otherwise let a teammate entry and trade.

## Practise economy calls

Detectors: economy_mismatch.
Before every buy, check the team's money and say the call: full buy, force or save. Match the call even when you could afford more, and write down after the match every round where your buy differed from the team's.

## Practise rotating on time

Detectors: late_rotation.
Watch your deaths and losses on a site you left from the radar and note when the first information arrived. In matches, decide your rotation route before the round starts, and move on the first call rather than waiting for a second one.

## Practise varying positions

Detectors: repeated_death_zone.
List the spots where you died more than once in this match. For each one, learn one other position that covers the same area, and switch between them from round to round so the enemy cannot prefire you.
